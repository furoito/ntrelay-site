Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2, 2026

NT Relay processes information needed to receive, review, route, verify, and return results for bounded external-execution requests.

Information we process

  • Request content, including objective, location when provided, deadline, constraints, evidence requirements, and optional budget ceiling.
  • Operational metadata such as timestamps, request status, source fingerprints, service logs, and security/rate-limit signals.
  • Completion evidence supplied for an approved task, which may include structured observations and permitted photos.

How we use information

We use it to operate the service, review requests for safety and feasibility, perform or coordinate approved work, provide status and results, prevent abuse, debug incidents, and improve reliability.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers or human operators only to the extent reasonably necessary to review or fulfill an approved request. We do not sell request data to advertisers.

Storage and security

Request access credentials are handled as capability tokens and are not intentionally stored in plaintext. Evidence storage is private and completed-request evidence is exposed to authorized requesters through time-limited access URLs.

Retention

During the pilot, records are retained only as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, debugging, dispute handling, and legal obligations. Retention practices may change as the service matures.

営業メール経由のアクセス計測

NT Relayの営業案内メールには、アクセス測定用の識別リンクが含まれる場合があります。リンクへのアクセス時刻、ページ表示が確認された時刻、デモ操作の有無を、企業別の営業活動の効果測定のため記録します。識別情報は当社の送信先企業の記録と照合する場合がありますが、実際の閲覧者個人を特定するものではありません。メールアドレスやIPアドレス、Cookie、ブラウザ指紋は、この独自計測記録には保存しません。セキュリティソフトによる自動アクセスも計上されることがあります。

上記の独自計測データは最大90日間保管し、その後削除します。計測の停止などのお問い合わせは、サイトの連絡窓口へお知らせください。

Your choices

Do not include unnecessary personal or sensitive information in requests. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us through the support page.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
